EchoScript Android preview 0.5.0
================================
Android 10 or later. Use synthetic, non-sensitive recordings for evaluation.
BETA GATE: NOT PASSED. This package uses a debuggable development build.

Official download
https://github.com/TwoWetKnuckles/echoscript/releases/tag/android-preview-0.5.0

Install on the device
1. Download EchoScript-offline-preview.apk for direct installation. You can
   also download EchoScript-Android-preview.zip for the complete package. In
   Android Files, extract its EchoScript-Android folder.
2. Open the downloaded or extracted APK and tap Install. Follow
   the Android prompt. If Android requests permission to install from Files,
   allow that source only if your device and organization permit it. Restore
   the permission setting afterward. If device policy blocks the install,
   use an approved test device.
3. Open EchoScript. In Cases, choose Load synthetic speech sample to try the
   workflow or Choose audio file to import your own synthetic recording. The
   speech models are included. All processing runs inside the app.
4. Analyze shows the waveform automatically after import. Select a speech
   candidate or keep the full recording, choose a filter preset and gain, then
   tap Render & transcribe. Render enhanced revision saves WAV separately.
   Settings saves the examiner identifier and Dark, Light or System appearance.
5. In Transcript, listen at the timestamps. Open Edit and verify segment to
   review words and draft speaker groups. Save
   examiner revisions when needed. Use Audit to verify. Export writes WAV,
   transcripts, reports or a case package to a destination you choose.
6. For a protected transfer, save revisions, then choose Create device-signed
   checkpoint in Export (also available in Audit). Retain the public-key
   fingerprint independently. Enter and confirm an unpredictable passphrase
   of at least 16 characters, acknowledge export handling and choose Protected
   case package. Show/Hide can help check typing; visibility resets after
   rotation/navigation. Keep the passphrase and completed package SHA-256
   separately. There is no password reset.
7. To receive a protected case, choose Transfer case in Cases, enter its
   passphrase and independently received hash, then select the package.
   In Audit, supply the independently trusted signer fingerprint to verify
   checkpoint coverage. Device signatures do not authenticate examiner identity.

Verification and upgrades
SHA256SUMS.txt contains the APK hash. Compare it to the value on the release
page or a separately trusted copy before installing. BUILD.txt identifies the
source commit and CI run. APK signing verifies the installer; case verification
has its own hashes and custody records.

If Android rejects an update to an earlier preview, the signing identities
may differ. Preserve the original and export any cases to protected storage
before removing or resetting an earlier preview. Uninstalling can erase the
vault. Test the export and independently preserve its SHA-256 before relying on
it. A fresh preview uses its own local vault. Preview 0.4.0 exports were plaintext
and unsigned. Preview 0.5.0 adds protected transfers and optional device-signed
historical checkpoints. Individual files and legacy ZIPs remain plaintext.
New edits after a checkpoint require a new checkpoint to cover those revisions.
Verify an exported snapshot before relying on it for recovery; no automatic
vault backup or resumable-job recovery is provided.
