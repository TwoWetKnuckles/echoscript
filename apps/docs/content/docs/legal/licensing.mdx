## Redistribution and additional promises

Comply with the full license, including retaining required notices, supplying the license, marking modified files and handling applicable NOTICE attribution. Do not remove another contributor's attribution or imply that Apache-2.0 grants trademark endorsement. This overview does not replace the license text.

Sections 7–8 contain the warranty and liability provisions and their exceptions. Section 9 addresses obligations a redistributor chooses to offer: support, warranties or other additional liability must not be accepted on other contributors' behalf. The project offers no separate service-level or ongoing maintenance commitment merely through publication.

## Third-party components and case data

Dependencies, fonts, codecs, speech-model weights and external services keep their respective licenses/terms. The bundled Noto Sans font includes its [SIL Open Font License](https://github.com/TwoWetKnuckles/echoscript/blob/feature/clearvoice-whisper-beta/apps/web/assets/OFL.txt). The pinned Vosk model's qualification and sources are in the [provider record](/docs/providers/local-vosk); the Android build fetches and bundles two pinned model archives with their license notice. The browser loopback setup fetches the English model separately. The synthetic fixture has a committed [provenance record](https://github.com/TwoWetKnuckles/echoscript/blob/feature/clearvoice-whisper-beta/specs/fixtures/synthetic-speech.provenance.json). These examples are not a complete third-party license audit.

Before distributing a build or adding an encoder/model, review the exact component/version's license, attribution, redistribution and applicable patent obligations. Preserve its notices in the distribution. Do not assume that a package's presence or this repository's Apache license clears every codec, model, service or use case.

The software license does not grant rights in imported recordings, transcripts, case materials or third-party voices. Users must establish the authority and permissions for those materials separately. Never submit real evidence or secrets as a contribution. Intentionally submitted contributions are handled under Apache section 5 unless an applicable separate agreement states otherwise; contributors must have the authority to submit them. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](/docs/development/contributing).
