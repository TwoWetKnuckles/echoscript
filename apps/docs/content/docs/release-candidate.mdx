A successful emulator or speech subset is recorded as that subset. A defect resets the relevant consecutive workflow count after repair. Outstanding physical-device, signing, policy and feature gates continue to block a release-candidate or beta claim.

## Evidence controls and remaining acceptance

Android includes opt-in streaming protected transfers, Keystore P-256 historical checkpoints and independent JVM/Node verification. See [the format and trust boundaries](/docs/evidence/protected-transfer). These controls do not establish examiner identity, revocation infrastructure, trusted time or an approved recovery policy. Current and historical executed results are separated in [QA status](/docs/qa/status).

[Acceptance execution instructions](/docs/qa/rc-acceptance) and the all-unpassed template record define the remaining evidence. `tools/release-gates.mjs` fails closed for missing gates, mismatched source/APK, changed referenced files, incomplete/subset workflows, runs before prerequisite approval, or fewer than seven consecutive complete results. It checks declared records and file consistency, not the truth or legal sufficiency of reviewer declarations. [Owner signing setup](/docs/android/owner-signing) prepares a non-debug evaluation artifact only; owner key provision and upgrade/MDM acceptance remain outstanding.

The remaining acceptance column above continues to apply. No row becomes passed simply because a control or test harness exists. Physical-device measurements, complete native feature parity, team authorization/sync, owner production signing and deployment-specific governance remain mandatory.
